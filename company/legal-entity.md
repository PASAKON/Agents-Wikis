## Tax / penalty math (as of 2026-08-03, pending the duration question above)

- **Actual corporate income tax owed (ภ.ง.ด.50): ฿0** — company has had
  no revenue per CEO (2-3 years), so no net profit → no tax base,
  regardless of how late the filing is.
- **DBD late financial-statement fine**: tiered by lateness, but **caps at
  ฿12,000 per unfiled year** (>4 months late = max tier, does not keep
  growing beyond that no matter how many additional months/years pass).
  Source: [simplebalance.co.th DBD fine table 2569](https://simplebalance.co.th/blog/dbd-fine-2569-calculation-reduction-ways/).
- **ภ.ง.ด.50 late-filing fine**: flat ฿2,000 per year (Revenue Code มาตรา 35),
  no เงินเพิ่ม since no tax is owed.
- **Estimated total fine exposure**: **~฿14,000 if 1 year unfiled**,
  **~฿28,000 if 2 years unfiled** — need the duration question resolved to
  know which.
- Plus CPA audit + DBD filing service cost to actually close the books:
  ~฿7,000-10,000 per year of catch-up (market-rate research, same session).

## Open decision — keep entity alive vs. dissolve

CEO has not yet said whether MOONIEX CO., LTD. is still intended as an
active vehicle or is effectively a dormant shell. Recommended framing for
next time this comes up:
- **Keep + catch up**: pay the accumulated fine (~฿14-28k) + hire CPA to
  file the missing year(s) (~฿7-10k/yr).
- **Dissolve (เลิกกิจการ)**: still need to clear outstanding fines/filings
  first (can't exit clean while overdue), but stops future annual
  fine/audit-cost accrual if the entity has no ongoing business purpose.
- Risk of doing nothing: DBD can administratively strike off (ร้าง) a
  chronically non-filing company; director's name stays attached to the
  compliance gap in the meantime.
